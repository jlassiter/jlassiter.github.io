About Me

Hey, I'm Joel! I'm a software engineer who genuinely loves what I do — whether that's building full-stack applications, wiring up infrastructure, or figuring out why something broke at 2am. I've been doing this for a long time and I still get excited about it.

I grew up in the south, bouncing between Georgia and the Carolinas, before making the move to New York City in 1999. NYC has been home ever since, and I can't imagine being anywhere else. The energy, the food, the sheer variety of people and perspectives — it keeps things interesting and keeps me sharp.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm usually spending time with my family, fussing over my dogs, digging around in the garden, or planning the next trip. Life's too short not to explore.
